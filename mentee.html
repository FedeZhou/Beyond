01Who it’s for

For students ready to go further.

Beyond is for Italian-Chinese talents aged 18 or over, at university or just starting their career, who are drawn to engineering, mathematics, physics, medicine, research, finance or any other challenging path. Maybe no one in your family has ever taken this road before. That is exactly why we are here.

What you get

  • A mentor from a similar background who has already walked the path you are considering
  • Honest advice on choosing a university, finding scholarships and preparing applications
  • Opportunities, programmes and people you might not otherwise hear about
  • A community of peers who are aiming as high as you are

What happens nextWe read every application personally. We will get to know you a little better by email, and then match you with a mentor whose path fits your goals.
